# Synthacks-2026-final for rcb
🌟 What is Health 360?
🩺 Health 360 is an interactive AI-powered healthcare web application designed to make general health information easier to access and understand.
🤖 It uses AI-based assistance to help users ask health-related questions and receive clear, easy-to-understand informational responses.
👤 Users can create an account and build a personal profile, allowing the application to organize information they choose to provide.
📋 Users can share relevant information with the application so that their experience can be more personalized and useful.
🔎 Instead of searching through multiple websites and trying to understand complicated medical terminology, users can access general health information through one centralized platform.
💬 The interactive assistant provides a conversational way for users to explore health-related topics and learn more about them.
🎯 The application focuses on accessibility, simplicity, personalization, and responsible use of AI.
⚠️ Health 360 clearly informs users that AI-generated information is not a medical diagnosis or prescription and should not replace professional medical advice.
🔐 Because health information can be sensitive, the project also emphasizes the importance of privacy and responsible handling of user information.
💡 The Problem
🌐 Health information is spread across countless websites.
😵 Medical information can be difficult for ordinary users to understand.
🔍 Searching for symptoms online can produce overwhelming or conflicting information.
🧑‍⚕️ Professional medical advice may not always be immediately accessible for basic informational questions.
📱 Users often have to switch between different platforms for different healthcare-related needs.
🚀 Our Solution

Health 360 brings general health information and AI assistance into one simple platform.

🤖 Ask questions through the AI health assistant.
👤 Create and manage a personal profile.
📋 Provide relevant information for a more personalized experience.
💬 Receive understandable, conversational explanations.
⚠️ Get clear safety reminders about the limitations of AI-generated health information.
🧭 Navigate healthcare information through a single, user-friendly interface.
