🩺 About Health 360
Health 360 is an AI-powered healthcare web app designed to make general health information simple, accessible, and easy to understand.
👤 Users can create an account and personal profile and provide information they choose to share.
🤖 An AI health assistant allows users to ask health-related questions and receive clear, easy-to-understand informational responses.
🔎 The app brings health information into one centralized platform, reducing the need to search through multiple websites.
🎯 It focuses on simplicity, personalization, accessibility, and responsible use of AI.
🔐 User information is treated as sensitive, with privacy and security considered important parts of the platform.
⚠️ Health 360 is not a doctor or diagnostic tool; its information is for general educational purposes and does not replace professional medical advice.
🚀 Future versions could include health tracking, medication reminders, appointment assistance, multilingual support, and doctor/hospital discovery.
💙 Our goal: Use AI and modern web technology to make understanding health information easier, smarter, and more accessible to everyone.
