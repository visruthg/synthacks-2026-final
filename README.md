# Synthacks-2026-final for rcb
Health 360 is an interactive web-based healthcare assistance platform designed to make accessing and understanding general health information simpler, faster, and more convenient. The application brings several useful healthcare features together in one easy-to-use interface, allowing users to create an account, build a personal profile, and provide information that they choose to share with the system so that their experience can be more personalized. At the heart of Health 360 is an AI-powered health assistant that allows users to ask questions about health, symptoms, common conditions, wellness, and other health-related topics and receive clear, easy-to-understand informational responses. Rather than requiring users to search through numerous websites and interpret complicated medical terminology on their own, Health 360 aims to present information in a more organized and accessible way. The platform also emphasizes responsible use of AI by clearly informing users that its responses are for general informational and educational purposes and should not be considered a medical diagnosis, prescription, or replacement for a qualified healthcare professional. By combining an intuitive user interface, personalized user profiles, and AI-powered assistance, Health 360 aims to demonstrate how modern web technology and artificial intelligence can be used to improve access to understandable health information while keeping safety and responsible use at the center of the experience. The project is designed with future expansion in mind, with possibilities such as health tracking, appointment assistance, medication reminders, multilingual support, emergency guidance, and connections to verified healthcare services. Ultimately, Health 360 is built around a simple idea: making general health information easier to access, understand, and navigate through one centralized digital platform.
